Sidewalk Devkit: nRF54L15 + nPM1300 + SX1262

01

Ship mode

The onboard nPM1300 uses “shipmode” to protect the battery from discharge when not in use. To wake the device, just press the top button labelled “BTN”. To enter ship mode, hold BTN for 5 seconds until blue flashes three times, then release.

02

LEDs

Blinks every 10 seconds while charging.
Blinks once when a packet is sent and twice when a packet is received over Sidewalk.
Breathes when waiting for shell connection. After connection, blinks once for sent messages and twice for received messages.
Blinks when the accelerometer detects movement. If this LED blinks when you shake the DK, it’s working!
